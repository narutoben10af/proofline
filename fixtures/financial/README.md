# Source-backed FY2025 financial fixtures

This pack contains small, project-owned derivatives of audited issuer facts for
Apple Inc. and PETRONAS Chemicals Group Berhad (PCG). It intentionally excludes
issuer PDFs and workbooks. The facts remain attributable to their issuers; this
repository's MIT licence does not imply an issuer licence or endorsement.

## What is safe to redistribute

- `normalized_facts.csv` contains a minimal set of reported numerical facts,
  source URLs, and pinpoint anchors. Every row is marked `copied` because the
  number is transcribed from a primary source. The CSV as a collection and its
  normalization metadata are project-owned.
- `expected_metrics.json` contains project-derived calculations from those
  facts. Every output is marked `derived_calculation`.
- `hero_cases.json` contains deterministic demo classifications and caveats.
- `sources.json` records source provenance and reproducible retrieval paths.

No downloaded issuer PDF or issuer workbook is committed. Retrieve sources at
setup time from the recorded official URLs and respect the issuer's terms and,
for SEC endpoints, the SEC fair-access policy.

## Metric registry (`proofline.financial.v1`)

All arithmetic uses decimal inputs and returns a decimal ratio. Percentages are
ratios multiplied by 100 only for display.

| Metric ID | Tier | Formula | Denominator and applicability policy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `revenue_growth_yoy` | 0 | `revenue_t / revenue_t_minus_1 - 1` | Same issuer, group scope, currency, unit, and comparable annual periods; prior revenue must be positive. |
| `operating_margin` | 0 | `operating_profit_or_loss / revenue` | Same annual period and group scope; revenue must be positive. A reported loss remains negative. |
| `current_ratio` | 0 | `current_assets / current_liabilities` | Same reporting instant and group scope; current liabilities must be positive. |
| `fcf_margin` | 0 | `(net_cash_from_operating_activities - cash_purchases_of_ppe) / revenue` | Project-defined non-GAAP metric. Cash purchases of PPE are stored as a positive cash-outflow magnitude before subtraction. Same annual period and group scope; revenue must be positive. |
| `gross_margin` | 1 | `gross_profit / revenue` | Optional stretch output included because both issuers report clean, comparable group inputs for FY2025. |

Inventory days is deliberately omitted: it is not needed for the demo, and
including another derived convention would add more interpretation than value.

## Source anchors and caveats

### Apple FY2025

Primary source: Apple FY2025 Form 10-K, filed 31 October 2025. The PDF viewer's
page number is one greater than Apple's printed Form 10-K page in the statement
section.

- Revenue and operating income: PDF page 31 / printed page 29,
  **Consolidated Statements of Operations**, rows `Total net sales` and
  `Operating income`, FY2025 and FY2024 columns.
- Current assets and current liabilities: PDF page 33 / printed page 30,
  **Consolidated Balance Sheets**, rows `Total current assets` and
  `Total current liabilities`, 27 September 2025 column.
- Operating cash flow and PPE cash purchases: PDF page 35 / printed page 32,
  **Consolidated Statements of Cash Flows**, rows `Cash generated by operating
  activities` and `Payments for acquisition of property, plant and equipment`,
  FY2025 column.
- Gross profit: PDF page 31 / printed page 29, **Consolidated Statements of
  Operations**, row `Gross margin`, FY2025 column. Apple calls the reported
  gross-profit subtotal “Gross margin”; Proofline preserves the source label.

All amounts are USD millions and consolidated. FY2025 and FY2024 each span 52
weeks, ending 27 September 2025 and 28 September 2024. Apple notes that certain
prior-period amounts in the notes were reclassified and that the 2025 segment
disclosure standard was adopted retrospectively; neither caveat changes the
primary-statement inputs used here.

Apple's official filing hub exposes an Excel download, but this fixture does not
depend on opaque workbook cell addresses. The committed rows are sourced to the
audited filing tables above, while `sources.json` records the reproducible
workbook retrieval route. A future workbook adapter may add verified cell
addresses without changing the metric inputs.

### PCG FY2025

Primary source: PCG Financial Report 2025, approved 23 February 2026. The
interactive PDF uses two printed pages per PDF page in this section.

- Current assets/current liabilities: PDF page 6 / printed page 10,
  **Statements of Financial Position**, Group 2025 column.
- Revenue, gross profit, and operating loss: PDF page 6 / printed page 11,
  **Statements of Profit or Loss**, Group 2025 and 2024 columns.
- Operating cash flow and PPE cash purchases: PDF page 10 / printed pages
  18–19, **Statements of Cash Flows**, Group 2025 column. Note 3 on PDF page 25
  / printed page 49 independently reconciles the RM2,082 million cash purchase
  to RM2,448 million of PPE additions and non-cash adjustments.

All amounts are RM millions and consolidated. PCG reports on a calendar-year
basis ending 31 December and rounds financial information to the nearest RM
million. The report says certain 2024 receivable and payable note comparatives
were reclassified to conform to the current presentation; the primary-statement
totals used here are presented as the audited FY2024 comparatives. No FX
conversion is performed.

## Genuine demo exception and evidence discipline

`pcg_operating_margin_swing` is the hero exception. Reported group revenue fell
from RM30,671 million to RM27,480 million, while operating profit of RM2,057
million became an operating loss of RM1,372 million. Deterministic calculations
show operating margin moving from 6.706661% to -4.992722%, a change of
-11.699383 percentage points. This is an exception, not a claim about cause.

The PCG media release says FY2025 revenue was RM27.5 billion. The audited value
is RM27.480 billion, which rounds to RM27.5 billion at the release's precision.
The fixture classifies that claim as `supported`, not `contradicted`. A claimed
cause, an issuer-defined FCF figure, or an unverified workbook cell is treated
as insufficient evidence rather than inferred.

## Verification

From the repository root, run:

```sh
python3 scripts/verify_financial_fixtures.py
```

The script uses only the Python standard library. It validates required
provenance, period/unit/scope compatibility, sign conventions, formula results,
hero-case calculations, and cross-file references. It never downloads sources.
